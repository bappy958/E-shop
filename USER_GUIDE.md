# Unique Collection 4.0 - Customer User Guide

Welcome to **Unique Collection 4.0**! This shopper's guide explains how to browse, select luxury fashion attire, use the AI Fashion Assistant, and complete orders.

---

## 🌐 1. Language Selection (বাংলা & English)

You can toggle the website language between Bengali (বাংলা) and English at any time by clicking the language button in the top right corner of the header.

---

## 👗 2. Browsing & Filtering Catalog

1. Click **"শপ (Shop)"** in the top navigation bar.
2. Use the left sidebar filter to narrow down items:
   - **Categories**: Select Panjabi, Executive Shirts, Exclusive Abaya & Borka, Hijab, T-Shirts & Polo, or Girls Collection.
   - **Price Range Slider**: Filter by price in BDT (৳).
   - **Sale Filter**: Show discounted items only.
   - **Sort Dropdown**: Sort by Newest, Price Low to High, Price High to Low, or Top Rated.

---

## 🤖 3. Using the AI Personal Fashion Assistant

1. Click the floating **"AI Stylist"** button at the bottom right corner of the screen.
2. Type a message or click suggested prompt chips (e.g., *"I need a luxury Panjabi for Eid morning under BDT 4000"*).
3. The AI assistant recommends matched items directly from our collection. Click any recommended item to open Quick View or add it to cart.

---

## 🛒 4. Cart & Checkout Process

1. On any product card, select your preferred size (M, L, XL, XXL) and color.
2. Click **"কার্টে যোগ করুন" (Add to Cart)**.
3. Open the right side Cart Drawer:
   - Apply coupon codes (e.g. `EID2026`).
   - Check the free shipping indicator bar.
4. Click **"চেকআউট করুন" (Proceed to Checkout)**:
   - Enter shipping details (Full Name, Phone Number, District, Upazila, and Address).
   - Choose payment method (**bKash**, **Nagad**, **Rocket**, Bank Transfer, or Cash on Delivery).
   - Place Order. You will receive a unique Tracking Number (e.g. `UC-2026-8819`).

---

## 🚚 5. Tracking Your Order

1. Click **"অর্ডার ট্র্যাক" (Track Order)** in header or footer.
2. Enter your Tracking Number (e.g., `UC-2026-8819`) or Phone Number.
3. View real-time 5-stage delivery progress (Order Placed -> Processing -> Shipped -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered).
